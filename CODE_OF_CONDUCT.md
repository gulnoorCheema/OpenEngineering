# Code of conduct

## How we work together

OpenEngineering welcomes people learning engineering, experienced practitioners, writers, researchers, artists, and developers. Treat people with respect regardless of experience, identity, background, or whether they use an AI tool.

Discuss the explanation or implementation, explain your reasoning, and help newcomers find a useful next step. Credit others' work, respect their boundaries, and accept corrections. Disagreement about a technical claim is welcome; attacks on the person making it are not.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are unacceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop disruptive behavior is also unacceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces the project maintains, and when someone represents OpenEngineering in an official project role. They do not make the maintainer responsible for unrelated independent communities or forks.

## Report a concern privately

Email [gulnoorcheema@gmail.com](mailto:gulnoorcheema@gmail.com).

Include relevant links, what happened, and how to contact you if you want a response. Share only information needed to understand the concern. Do not post a sensitive report in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities have a separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The repository maintainer handles reports with discretion and shares details only as needed to assess or address the issue. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, including where disclosure is required by law. This volunteer project cannot promise a fixed response time or an independent appeals panel. If a concern involves the maintainer, GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for conduct that violates GitHub's policies.

## Response and enforcement

The maintainer will consider the context, severity, and any repeated behavior before responding. Responses may include requesting a correction or apology, warning someone, removing harmful content, limiting participation temporarily, or banning participation. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate action. Where appropriate, explain the decision privately and allow the affected person to supply relevant corrections or context. Do not retaliate against a reporter or publish their report without permission.
