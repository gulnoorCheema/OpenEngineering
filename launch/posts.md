## Engine clip

How does a straight push become a circle?

I built a small engine you can slow down, take apart, and explore in 3D. Follow the piston, then see why one power stroke takes two turns of the crank.

Free to explore, and the code is open. Built with GPT-6 Astra.

https://gulnoorcheema.github.io/OpenEngineering/exhibits/engine/?chapter=make-a-circle&phase=90

Attach `assets/openengineering-engine.mp4`.

## Differential clip
