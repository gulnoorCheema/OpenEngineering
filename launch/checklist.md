- [ ] Resolve the open eligibility questions in `eligibility.md`.

- [ ] Verify the production URL and all three exhibits.
- [ ] Review the maker comment and actual recording exports.
- [ ] Complete ten observed beginner sessions using the worksheet.
- [ ] Have an unfamiliar contributor test the guide.
- [ ] Obtain a mechanical review, or retain the accurate source-reviewed-only description.
- [ ] Schedule Product Hunt for September 18, 2026, 12:01 a.m. Pacific.

## September 17

- [ ] Confirm Product Hunt's actual scheduled status.
- [ ] Prepare direct links and community-specific posts.
- [ ] Check the static host's usage and keep a portable build archive.

## Launch day

- [ ] Check production after the listing goes live.
- [ ] Publish the prepared actual-product demo.
- [ ] Answer specific questions and acknowledge defects honestly.
- [ ] Record engagement and learning feedback separately from votes and stars.

## Following week

- [ ] Address the most common beginner misunderstanding.
- [ ] Help the first independent contributor land a focused change.
- [ ] Choose the next exhibit from observed interest and technical feasibility.

Targets, not results: 100 completed stories in 48 hours; ten substantive feedback items; one merged external contribution during the first week.
